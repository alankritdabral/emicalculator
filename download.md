# Image Download & Share Workflow

Since the images are now downloaded individually with timestamped filenames (e.g., `image_1_2026-09-04_15-32-15.png`), the process of sharing them varies slightly depending on your operating system and device. Here is the step-by-step flow for each major platform:

## 💻 Desktop (Windows & macOS)

**1. Download**
* Click the **Download Image 1**, **Download Image 2**, and **Download Image 3** buttons.
* Your web browser will automatically save these images to your default `Downloads` folder.

**2. Share via WhatsApp**
* Open [WhatsApp Web](https://web.whatsapp.com/) in your browser or launch the **WhatsApp Desktop App**.
* Open the chat where you want to share the presentation.
* **Option A (Drag & Drop):** Open your File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (macOS), select the latest 3 timestamped images from your Downloads folder, and drag them directly into the WhatsApp chat window.
* **Option B (Attach):** Click the **+** (or paperclip) icon in WhatsApp, select **Photos & Videos**, navigate to your Downloads folder, select the images, and send.

---

## 📱 Android (Samsung, Pixel, etc.)

**1. Download**
* Tap the three download buttons one by one.
* Your browser (like Chrome) will show a popup confirming that the files were downloaded.

**2. Share via WhatsApp**
* Open the **WhatsApp** app and go to your desired chat.
* Tap the **paperclip** (attachment) icon in the message box.
* Tap **Gallery**. You will usually find the downloaded images right at the top under **Recents**.
* Select the three images in order, add any captions if needed, and hit send.

---

## 🍏 iOS (iPhone & iPad)

**1. Download**
* Tap the download buttons. If using Safari, a small popup will ask, "Do you want to download this file?". Tap **Download**.
* The files are saved to the **Downloads** folder inside the Apple **Files** app. 

**2. Share via WhatsApp**
* Open **WhatsApp** and navigate to your chat.
* Tap the **+** icon next to the text input box.
* Since Safari saves downloads as files, tap **Document**.
* Tap **Choose from Files** (or just browse your Recents).
* Select the three downloaded `image_X_...png` files, tap **Open**, and send them to your contact. 

> [!TIP]
> **Saving to iPhone Camera Roll:** If you prefer sending them as normal photos instead of documents on iOS, you can open the **Files** app, tap the downloaded images, tap the Share icon, and select **Save Image**. They will then appear in your normal Photo Library.
